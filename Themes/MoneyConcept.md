# Concept of money

There is no concept of money on Mars, the place is a bit like heaven. The girl wants to subdue the AI empire and re-introduce the concept of money. People work together and live a peaceful life. Place here is only offered to the deserving (It would look like that for sure). Whole process would be automated by AI survilliance system gathering info about everyone throughout their life but decisions are made by officials at the end.

People are given a purpose, a community driven, love doing something, skilled. It advertises itself as a place for great people who are destined to futher greatness.

AI thinks it can rule and bring random undeserving people (by falsefying data), frames them for murders and claims that it can do better if it in the decisive position.
