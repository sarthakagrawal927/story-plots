# Opening (For watching)

The opening scene will be set in present and an imaginary 2120 world is shown according to the girl's dream but is quickly distorted by showing the harsh reality of 2120.

Then we see people in 2120 discussing the issues of the world and how they got to there followed by the girl giving speech in her classroom about the future of artificial intelligence in 2020. Discussing about how robots are going to take an important place in world.

The scene then shows the male lead aka the supporting cast in 2120,  who is also fed up with his life in 2120 and is doing a minimal job. He is desperate to be the person chosen to go back to past to collect necessary information, but unfortunately his brother , cheats on him and goes to past instead of him.

He will become the major villain in the story later.
