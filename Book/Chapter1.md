# Intro

Soon everyone had discovered that the machines could go evil. Then there were murders. Some people went on a killing fest because they could blame it on artificial intelligence. While the lawyers of the AICorps were fighting their ass off to prevent any prosecution, just the possibility that machines can think and murder people had people panicking. But I am getting ahead of myself: let me dial it back a few years. The year is 2089, and humanity just launched its completely unmanned fleet of robots. It was marvellous, seeing a sky full of robots just for our service and protection.
