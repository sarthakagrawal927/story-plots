# 2 villains

There will be 2 villains : one (female daughter of main villain) that will be hated by everyone but won't pertain a very big threat. She will do small things badly , her main aim would to create chaos without reason. In her backstory she is supposed to be an evil student with incredibly high IQ. She is supposed to be a bully at school. She loved playing mind games and using them to cause quarrel and chaos. All this because of severe negligence in her childhood.

The other will be the main one (male), audience will be able to sympathize to him at the end. He will have one major destructing role in the whole story.  He is the mad scientist that was able to create an AI with conscious and merged it with his brain aiming to get extreme level of intelligence for his future endeavors. Things went north because of his daughter as she removed the code that was meant to control AI to take over her father's mind.  The old maid saw all this but was threatened by her. The maid will play a crucial role later in the story by helping our lead to hide.
