# XMEN

Due to a meteor explosions mutants start to appear but were weak (got either killed/ or hidden) after 2-3 generation of cross breeding a strong generation of mutants have now start to surface on Earth. Both the govt and AI knows about this and understands the threat and make sure that none of them ever reach Mars, but things hardly ever go as planned.

When aliens actually come and Earth is a bit unprepared XMEN step into action.
