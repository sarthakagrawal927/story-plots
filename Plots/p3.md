# Revolution of 2120

In distant future, 2116, the world is controlled, not ruled, by artificial intelligence . The scientists have finally figured out a way for time travel in the past by various experiments. They sent to robots in past  (2020) accompanied by a man in past to collect data. But the man was depressed with the present so he decided to break out of the loop and live freely in year 2020. He decides to destroy the robots and run away. But he fails to destroy the machine which was already scheduled to return in a month. Accidentally a curious girl (13) gets in the machine and is transported in the far future. In her present she was going to be the best. When she reaches in 2116 she is very quickly adopted by an extremely high class family as they were not able to conceive a child for years. She learns that life under Artificial Intelligence is doing more bad than good to humanity. No doubt that humans had came a long way in technology of all sorts because of AI but the very nature that made us humans was gone. She lives under this for 4 years while she completed here college as in future the education was only compulsory and required till the age of 17. She decides to revolt for that.
